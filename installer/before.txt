MD Doc Link v1.0.0
Author: MD (Mayank Dholariya)

A panel for Autodesk Navisworks Manage / Simulate 2017 to 2027 (64-bit).
Reads an Excel list (for example a line list or equipment list), matches each
row's key (line no. / tag) to model items, and adds the row's documents
(PDF, drawing, folder or web link) as Navisworks hyperlinks on those items.

Needs Navisworks Manage or Simulate. Navisworks Freedom cannot load plugins.

Please close Navisworks before continuing.
